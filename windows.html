Heads-up: the first time you open Kharcha, your computer will show a warning. That is normal for apps that aren't from an app store, and the steps below take about 30 seconds. You only do this once.
  1. Open the downloaded file Kharcha-mac-apple-silicon.dmg (it's in your Downloads folder), then drag Kharcha onto Applications.
  2. Open Kharcha from your Applications folder. A box says “Kharcha” Not Opened. Click Done.Don't click “Move to Bin”.
  3. Open Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security, then scroll down to the Security section. Next to “Kharcha” was blocked to protect your Mac, click Open Anyway and enter your Mac password (or use Touch ID). Click Open Anyway once more if asked.
  4. Kharcha opens in your web browser. From now on, just double-click it like any other app.
I don't see the “Open Anyway” button

It only appears for about an hour after you try to open the app. Double-click Kharcha in Applications again (click Done on the box), then go straight back to Privacy & Security and scroll down.

It says Kharcha is “damaged” or “can't be opened”

Delete the app from Applications (and the Bin), download it again with the button above, and repeat the steps. If it still fails, send Sylvin a screenshot.

How do I know if my Mac has an Apple chip?

Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says “Chip: Apple M…”, use the Mac download. If it says Intel, look for the “Older Intel Mac” link under the Mac button, or message Sylvin.

  1. Click the download button. If your browser says the file “isn't commonly downloaded” or “could harm your device”: Edge: click the … next to the file → Keep → Keep anyway.
    Chrome: click the ⋮ or arrow next to the file → Keep (then Keep anyway if asked).
  2. Open your Downloads folder and double-click Kharcha-windows.exe. The first start takes about 10 seconds. Please wait.
  3. A blue box says “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then click Run anyway.The “Run anyway” button only appears after you click “More info”.
  4. Kharcha opens in your web browser. From now on, just double-click it. You can drag it to your Desktop for easy access.

Give it 10–20 seconds the first time. If your browser still doesn't open, open http://localhost:8765 in your browser.

My antivirus deleted or blocked the file

New, unsigned apps are sometimes flagged by mistake. Restore it from the antivirus “quarantine” and choose “allow”, or ask Sylvin for help.

Questions

Is it safe?

Kharcha runs only on your own computer and reads only the statement files you give it. It has no accounts and no cloud, and it never sends your data anywhere. The warning appears because Apple and Microsoft charge a yearly fee to “verify” apps, and this one hasn't paid it yet.

How do I close it?

Close the browser tab. Kharcha stops by itself about a minute later. Or use Settings → Data & reset → Quit Kharcha inside the app.
